GNU write-back specialization, macOS/ARM64, 2026-11-18

The baseline is EAX_ESP write-through with the 448-entry GNU plan and budget
512. The candidate is WRITE_BACK with the 704-entry GNU write-back plan and
a single forward decode pass. Both use SHAPED fusion, HYBRID/ALL lowering,
MEMORY pin specialization, AppleClang Release ThinLTO, the same managed CLI
and the same guest executable/input per case. Timing is serialized and the
variant order rotates each round. No benchmark binary is rebuilt.

Candidate changes: retain the entire 448-entry plan and append 48 fused
plus 208 single-instruction shapes. Two added ADD reg,[ESP] shapes reached
262144 estimated executions each in the earlier SHA256 count profile. The
intermediate 704-entry candidate gained speed in gzip, gunzip, SHA256 and
sed, but left tar at -0.716% after three rounds. The final candidate does
fusion matching and operand lowering in one pass and only reverse-maps
entries marked as lowered before rebuilding concatenated blocks.
